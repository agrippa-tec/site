The company

What every Agrippa product guarantees

  1. An answer about your land. Every product speaks of a real place — field, farm, parcel.
  2. Science and field, both ways. Technical-scientific knowledge crosses with the knowledge of the people who work the land. Not everything is published — that's why both sides count.

  6. Data honesty. It shows what was measured or says it doesn't know — never guesses on your behalf.

The name

Agrippa made this

Agrippa gave Rome free water, opened Gaul's road network, won Antiquity's greatest naval battle through logistics and summed up the entire world in a map hung in public: the empire's scattered information turned into an image anyone could read. He wasn't about speeches, he was about works. And he signed: M·AGRIPPA·FECIT, still on the Pantheon's facade, beneath the world's largest concrete dome.

The company carries the name because it works the way he worked: it gathers the scattered information and returns it condensed into an answer for decision-makers. With the name underneath.
