Where the answer comes from

Four sources, one answer

Each problem calls for a combination of data, method and experience.

▾Remote sensing Observations of the land and its changes: vegetation, rain and heat spots.
▾Public data Territorial databases such as CAR, SIGEF and IBGE, considered with their dates, scales and limitations.
▾Technical-scientific knowledge Methods for analyzing information and recognizing its limits.
▾Field knowledge The observation of those who work the land helps shape questions and check answers.

The company

What guides our work

  1. A real place. Information tied to the land and to the decision of those who use it.
  2. Science and field. Technical knowledge and practical experience complement each other.
  3. Technology with purpose. We use AI where it makes the answer genuinely better. A tool at the service of the work, not decoration to impress.
  4. Simplicity with rigor. Simple to use, with the important details within reach.
  5. One central problem. Each feature must contribute to the main need.
  6. Clear limits. Distinguishing observations, estimates and uncertainties is part of the answer.

The name

Agrippa made this

Two thousand years ago, Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa brought water to Rome, organized the knowledge of the known world and signed what he built. The Pantheon he raised was rebuilt under Hadrian, and the inscription kept on the facade still carries his name.

The inspiration is in the work: understanding the land, gathering knowledge and building something useful. For Agrippa, signing a solution means taking responsibility for its clarity, its limits and its continuity.
