The name

Agrippa made this

Agrippa gave Rome free water, opened Gaul's road network, won Antiquity's greatest naval battle through logistics and summed up the entire world in a map displayed to the public. He wasn't about speeches, he was about works. And he inscribed his name on them: M·AGRIPPA·FECIT, on the Pantheon's facade, beneath what is still the world's largest concrete dome.

The name is the standard the company set for itself: engineering that gathers scattered information and returns it condensed into an answer for whoever decides. With the name underneath.
