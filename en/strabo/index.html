What it does

Opens, locates, works

Open and useGeoPDF becomes a map in seconds and stays in the library, always available without internet.
See where you areInstant GPS with accuracy circle, edge arrow and distance-to-border warning.
MarkersPins with name, description, colors and ~90 symbols; geofence with entry and exit alerts.
MeasurementsDistance and area with snapping to vertices, edges and intersections — like desktop GIS.
GPS trackRecords your route with the screen off, live trace on the map.
NavigationCompass and dashboard to a point; coordinates in decimal, DDM, DMS and UTM.
Base layersShapefile, GeoPackage, GeoJSON and KML over the map, with category colors and labels.
ExportKML/KMZ, GPX, GeoJSON, GeoPackage, Shapefile and Strabo package to share the whole job.
Real privacyNo account, no analytics, no ads. Everything stays on the device.

Why Strabo?

Strabo was the Greek geographer who described Agrippa's world map — Rome's engineer who measured the known world. The original map was lost; Strabo's description crossed two thousand years and reached us. The app does the same with your map: it takes it out of the cabinet and puts it in your pocket — where the work happens.
